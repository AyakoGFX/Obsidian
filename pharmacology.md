Sexual Health
- Dapoxetine – Used for premature ejaculation    
- Cialis (Tadalafil) – Used for erectile dysfunction

Hair Loss / Hair Growth
- Minoxidil – Promotes hair growth
- Finasteride – DHT blocker (hair loss treatment)
- Dutasteride – Stronger DHT blocker than finasteride

Acne Treatment
- Accutane (Isotretinoin) – Oral medication for severe acne

Skin / Anti-Aging / Pigmentation
- Tretinoin – Topical retinoid (acne and anti-aging)
- Hydroquinone – Skin lightening / hyperpigmentation treatment
- GHK-Cu – Copper peptide (skin repair, anti-aging, sometimes hair support)

Weight Loss / Metabolic
- Retatrutide – Investigational drug for obesity and metabolic disorders
-  Aqualix -  reduce localized, stubborn fat deposits
